In short

  • Your contacts and call details are processed on your phone and are never sent to our servers.

  • You can turn features off, withdraw permissions in Android settings, or ask us to delete your account data.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to every version of Netra Plus, including the Android app on Google Play and the desktop software for Microsoft Windows.

Both versions use the internet to sync your data securely through Firebase Cloud Firestore, including the 7-digit dynamic secret code used to connect the two. The Windows app may ask for standard system permissions, such as the microphone or storage, only to perform its features on your PC. Your personal files and screen content are not shared or stored on our servers.

2. Information we collect

Netra Plus collects only what it needs to work:

  • Basic app usage data, so features work as expected
  • Device information, such as your Android version
  • Account data, only if you choose to sign in with Google

We do not collect or store your name, phone number, address, contacts, call history or private messages on our servers. A few features read some of this information on your phone, only while you use them. Each one is described below.

When you use an AI feature, such as Image Analyser or Netra AI, the photo or text you choose is sent to Google Gemini only to get your answer. Google handles it under its own Privacy Policy.

3. Caller Announcer and your call information

Caller Announcer speaks the caller's name or number aloud while your phone rings, because a blind user cannot read the incoming call screen. It is off until you turn it on.

What it uses

  • Phone state (READ_PHONE_STATE), to know when a call is ringing, answered or ended.
  • Call log (READ_CALL_LOG), which Android requires before it gives an app the incoming caller's number.
  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS), to find the name saved for that number.

How it handles that information

  • The number is used on your phone only, to look up the name and speak it with text to speech.
  • Your call history, phone numbers and contacts are never stored by the app, uploaded to our servers or shared with anyone.
  • Announcements stop as soon as the call is answered, rejected or missed.
  • While announcing, the app may briefly lower your ringtone volume and then restore it.

You can turn Caller Announcer off at any time in the app, or remove these permissions in Android Settings › Apps › Netra Plus › Permissions.

4. Google Sign-In and account data

If you sign in with Google, we receive a unique user ID from Google. We use it only to:

  • Restore your app preferences
  • Keep your favorites and customization settings
  • Restore your ad-free subscription, if you have one

We never access your Google password, emails or contacts.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Netra Plus may offer an optional subscription that removes advertisements.

  • All payments are handled by Google Play Billing.
  • We never see or store your payment details.
  • Your subscription status is checked only to turn ads on or off.

6. Advertisements

Free users may see advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

  • Ads may use device identifiers as described in Google's policies.
  • Netra Plus does not share your personal data with advertisers.
  • Subscribers see no ads.

7. Permissions we use

Netra Plus asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can withdraw any permission in Android settings.

Camera
For Talking Camera guidance, text reading, scanning and other vision features.
Microphone
For voice commands and other voice-based features.
Phone, call log and contacts READ_PHONE_STATE, READ_CALL_LOG, READ_CONTACTS
Only for Caller Announcer, to speak who is calling. Processed on your phone and never stored or uploaded. See section 3.
All files access MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
To manage, organize and delete your OCR results, PDF documents and audio recordings across your phone's storage.
Internet
For online features and ads.
Accessibility service
To support TalkBack and screen reader users.

Why Netra Plus needs all files access. Netra File Manager is a core feature for our visually impaired users. It lets them manage, organize and delete OCR results, PDF documents and audio recordings anywhere on their phone, which standard scoped storage does not allow. All of this happens offline, on the device.

8. Accessibility commitment

Netra Plus is designed for accessibility first:

  • Full TalkBack support
  • Voice announcements through text to speech
  • No important action that needs a gesture only
  • Clear spoken feedback for every action

9. Data storage and security

  • Most data stays on your device.
  • Optional cloud data is stored securely with Google Firebase.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

10. Children's privacy

Netra Plus does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your choices

  • Turn any feature off, or withdraw its permission in Android settings.
  • Remove your favorites and settings at any time.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data.
  • Email us to request deletion of your account data.

12. Changes to this policy
