Privacy Policy

App Name: Netrax Plus – Accessible Smart Utilities

Developer: Netrax Innovations

Last Updated: August 2026

Netrax Plus is designed with a strong focus on accessibility, especially for blind and visually impaired users. Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

Netrax Plus collects only the minimum data required for the app to function correctly.

  • Basic app usage data (for feature functionality)
  • Device-related information (such as Android version)
  • Optional account data if you sign in using Google

We do NOT collect personal information such as your name, phone number, address, contacts, or private messages.

2. Google Sign-In & Account Data

If you choose to sign in using Google, we receive a unique user ID from Google. This ID is used only to:

  • Restore your app preferences
  • Maintain favorites and customization settings
  • Restore ad-removal subscription status (if applicable)

We do not access your Google password, emails, or contacts.

3. Subscription & Payments

Netrax Plus may offer an optional subscription to remove advertisements.

  • All payments are handled securely by Google Play Billing
  • We do not see or store payment details
  • Subscription status is checked only to enable or disable ads

4. Advertisements

Free users may see advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

  • Ads may use device identifiers as per Google policies
  • No personal data is shared by Netrax Plus directly
  • Paid users will see no ads

5. Permissions Used

Netrax Plus may request the following permissions:

  • Camera – for Talking Camera Guidance features
  • Microphone – for voice-based features
  • Storage / Media – to save photos, PDFs, and audio files
  • Internet – for online features and ads
  • Accessibility Services – to support TalkBack users

Permissions are used only when required and only for the related feature.

6. Accessibility Commitment

Netrax Plus is built for accessibility-first usage:

  • Full TalkBack support
  • Voice announcements using Text-to-Speech
  • No gesture-only critical actions
  • Clear spoken feedback for actions

7. Data Storage & Security

All user data is stored securely:

  • Local data stored on your device
  • Optional cloud data stored securely using Firebase
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties

8. Children’s Privacy

Netrax Plus does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe such data has been collected, please contact us immediately.

9. User Control

You can:

  • Remove favorites and settings at any time
  • Uninstall the app to remove local data
  • Contact us to request data deletion (if applicable)

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.
